Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Rang Pahadi Studio ("we") runs art workshops in Dehradun and this website, rangpahadistudio.in. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and the rights you have under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We collect only what we need, and we never sell your data.

Handing over these details is your choice, but we need your name and mobile number to manage a booking or entry.

Where your data is kept

Your details are stored in the studio’s Google account (Google Sheets and Google Drive), protected by its password and two-step sign-in. Google processes data for us under its own security and privacy terms. If you message us on WhatsApp, that conversation is also covered by WhatsApp’s terms. We do not share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Bookings, payments and vouchers: as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires.
  • Enquiries, custom-artwork requests and challenge entries: up to 12 months after our last contact, then deleted.
  • Reviews: while they are shown on the website, or until you ask us to remove them.
  • Google Analytics data: 2 months.

Children

Our workshops welcome children, but their details must come from a parent or guardian. A parent or guardian makes every booking for a child, and anyone under 18 entering the challenge must have a parent or guardian send the entry. We do not track children or show them targeted advertising.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it (unless the law requires us to keep it);
  • withdraw a consent you gave, for example to featuring your design or to analytics;
  • nominate someone to act for you if you are unable to.

Message the contact below. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Analytics choice: you can change your analytics choice at any time with the “Privacy choices” link at the bottom of the website.

Security

The website uses HTTPS. Forms are checked for misuse, our Google account uses two-step sign-in, and only the studio owners can open the data. If a breach ever affects your data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.
