Rang Pahadi Studio: art workshops in Dehradun

Rang Pahadi Studio is a hands-on art studio in Dehradun, Uttarakhand, run by artist Akanksha Singhal. Join a small-batch workshop, learn step by step and take home what you make. No experience needed, and all materials are included.

Workshops

  • Aipan art: the traditional Kumaoni folk art of Uttarakhand, painted on plates, wood and canvas.
  • Painting & drawing: watercolour, acrylic and paint & sip sessions for beginners.
  • Texture art & mixed media: palette knife, modelling paste and layered canvases.
  • Candle making: scented soy candles and decorated pillar candles.
  • Block printing & handicrafts: hand-block printed totes and cushion covers.
  • Kids' Art Club: weekly drawing, colour and craft classes for ages 6–12, plus winter art camps.

Weekend workshops start from ₹1,100 per person. The Kids' Art Club is ₹3,000 a month. Private sessions for birthdays, kitty parties, schools and corporate teams are available from 8 people.

Custom artwork and gifts

Order a custom painting, Aipan piece, hand-painted nameplate, plate or candle gift set. Gift vouchers and workshop passes are also available.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any art experience? No. Every workshop starts from the basics.

What is included? All materials: paints, canvas, wax, brushes and aprons.

How do I pay? By UPI after booking. Your seat is confirmed once the payment arrives.
