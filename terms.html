Terms, cancellations & refunds

Last updated: 28 November 2026

These terms apply when you book a workshop, a Makery table, a gift voucher or a custom artwork with Rang Pahadi Studio, run by Akanksha Singhal at MDDA HIG Society, Near ISBT, Haridwar Bypass Road, Dehradun, Uttarakhand 248171.

Prices and payment

  • All prices are in Indian rupees and include all materials, unless the workshop says otherwise.
  • Pay by UPI using the QR code or UPI ID shown after booking. Your seat is confirmed once the payment reaches us.
  • Unpaid bookings may be released after 24 hours so others can book.

Changing or cancelling a booking

  • Up to 48 hours before the workshop: move to another date free of charge.
  • Less than 48 hours before: we can’t refund, but you’re welcome to send a friend in your place.
  • If we cancel or reschedule a workshop, you choose a full refund or a seat on another date.
  • Refunds go back to the UPI account you paid from, within 7 working days.

The Makery

Each Makery project has one price per person, materials included. Children dropped off may stay up to 2 hours; extra time after their project is charged at ₹149 per half hour. Drop-off rules shown on the website apply.

Gift vouchers and passes

  • A voucher or pass becomes active once its payment is confirmed.
  • It can be used towards any workshop, can be passed on to someone else, and cannot be exchanged for cash.

Custom artwork

  • We confirm the price, size and timeline on WhatsApp before starting.
  • A 50% advance starts the work. If you cancel before we begin, the advance is refunded in full; once work has started it is not refundable. The balance is paid on delivery.
  • Small colour and size variations are part of handmade work.

At the studio

  • Children under the age shown for a workshop must be accompanied by an adult.
  • Please tell us about allergies or health needs when you book.
  • We may take photos of finished pieces. We post people’s faces only with their permission.
